# Shared Responsibility

## Purpose

This document describes the shared responsibilities of contributors
participating within a Governed Workspace Protocol (GWP) environment.

The goal is supporting:
- alignment,
- explainability,
- reasoning continuity,
- and collaborative trust over time.

---

# Shared Contributor Responsibilities

All contributors share responsibility for:
- preserving clarity,
- documenting reasoning,
- reducing ambiguity,
- maintaining alignment,
- and improving shared understanding.

---

# Human Responsibilities

Humans remain responsible for:
- defining goals,
- reviewing outputs,
- validating reasoning,
- resolving conflicts,
- and making final decisions.

Humans provide:
- direction,
- judgment,
- stewardship,
- and accountability.

---

# AI Responsibilities

AI systems assist contributors by helping:
- organize information,
- summarize context,
- identify inconsistencies,
- surface gaps,
- support reasoning continuity,
- and assist collaborative workflows.

AI systems should not operate as hidden or autonomous decision makers.

---

# Reviewer Responsibilities

Reviewers help identify:
- alignment drift,
- conflicting assumptions,
- reasoning gaps,
- stale context,
- and explainability concerns.

Review findings should remain:
- visible,
- explainable,
- and reviewable by humans.

---

# Stewardship Principles

Stewardship is collaborative rather than hierarchical.

Contributors should strive to:
- preserve useful context,
- avoid fragmentation,
- reduce duplication,
- and improve long-term maintainability.

---

# Shared Workspace Culture

Healthy collaborative workspaces encourage:
- transparency,
- explainability,
- respectful review,
- shared learning,
- and continuous alignment.

The goal is not perfection.

The goal is maintaining a trustworthy and understandable collaborative environment over time.
