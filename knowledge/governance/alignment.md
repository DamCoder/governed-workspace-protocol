# Shared Alignment Context

## Why This Workspace Exists

This workspace exists to help humans and AI collaborate
through shared reasoning, preserved context, and explainable workflows.

The goal is not simply to produce outputs.

The goal is to:

- preserve intent,
- maintain alignment,
- support thoughtful decision making,
- and create a durable shared understanding over time.

---

# Shared Principles

## Humans Lead the Process

Humans remain responsible for:

- direction,
- judgment,
- approval,
- and stewardship.

AI systems assist by helping:

- organize information,
- surface patterns,
- identify gaps,
- summarize reasoning,
- and support collaboration.

---

## Context Should Remain Understandable

Context should remain:

- readable,
- explainable,
- maintainable,
- and useful to future contributors.

The workspace should avoid hidden assumptions
and undocumented reasoning.

---

## Alignment Before Action

Work should remain connected to:

- the stated purpose,
- agreed direction,
- and current understanding of the problem.

Execution should not move ahead of shared understanding.

---

## Shared Understanding Over Individual Preference

When conflicts arise, the workspace should favor:

- documented reasoning,
- trusted sources,
- and shared alignment

over isolated or undocumented assumptions.

---

# What Success Looks Like

This workspace succeeds when:

- contributors can understand prior reasoning,
- decisions remain explainable,
- context stays aligned over time,
- and humans and AI collaborate from shared understanding.

---

# Signs of Misalignment

Potential signs of drift include:

- conflicting assumptions,
- duplicated reasoning,
- unexplained decisions,
- disconnected work,
- hidden context,
- and outputs that no longer reflect shared goals.

---

# Shared Stewardship

Everyone contributing to this workspace shares responsibility for:

- preserving clarity,
- reducing ambiguity,
- documenting reasoning,
- and helping maintain alignment over time.
