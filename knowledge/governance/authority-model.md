# Trusted Sources

## Purpose

This document describes how contributors should think about:

- trust,
- authority,
- source precedence,
- and conflict resolution within the workspace.

The goal is not rigid control.

The goal is helping contributors maintain aligned,
explainable, and trustworthy collaboration.

---

# General Source Priority

When conflicts or uncertainty occur,
contributors should generally prioritize:

1. Shared Alignment Context ([[alignment]])
2. Approved Workspace Documents
3. Reviewed Research and Evidence
4. Collaborative Review Findings
5. Current Working Context
6. AI Suggestions and Generated Content
7. Personal Preference or Assumptions

---

# Trusted Source Principles

## Documented Understanding Over Hidden Assumptions

Contributors should favor:

- documented reasoning,
- shared understanding,
- and explainable context

over isolated or undocumented assumptions.

---

## Reviewed Context Over Unreviewed Context

Reviewed and collaboratively validated context should generally
be prioritized over:

- unreviewed notes,
- temporary assumptions,
- or disconnected working material.

---

## Human Direction Over Autonomous Action

Humans remain responsible for:

- approval,
- interpretation,
- prioritization,
- and final decisions.

AI systems assist collaboration but do not replace human stewardship.

---

## Workspace-Specific Authority

Individual workspaces may define additional trusted sources
or authority relationships appropriate to their domain.

GWP defines collaboration principles,
not domain-specific authority structures.

---

# Conflict Resolution

When contributors encounter conflicting information,
review should focus on:

- explainability,
- shared understanding,
- documented reasoning,
- and alignment with workspace purpose.

Unresolved conflicts should remain visible rather than hidden.
