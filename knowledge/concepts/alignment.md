# Alignment

The ongoing state of work remaining connected to:

- shared purpose,
- documented reasoning,
- trusted sources,
- and current understanding.

When alignment is lost, the result is [context drift](context-drift.md). The
workspace-level expression of this concept is the
[Shared Alignment Context](../governance/alignment.md).
