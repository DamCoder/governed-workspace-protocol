# Review History

## Purpose

The `REVIEW_HISTORY` folder preserves review lineage and reasoning continuity over time.

Review history may include:
- machine-generated review findings,
- human review updates,
- alignment discussions,
- drift investigations,
- or final review outcomes.

---

# Goals

Review history exists to support:
- explainability,
- transparency,
- alignment tracking,
- and long-term collaborative understanding.

The goal is not maintaining audit bureaucracy.

The goal is preserving reasoning continuity and visible alignment evolution.

---

# Suggested Review Record Structure

Review records may include:
- review date,
- review scope,
- findings,
- severity,
- recommendations,
- human updates,
- and final resolution status.

---

# Human and AI Collaboration

Review history may contain:
- AI-generated observations,
- reviewer summaries,
- human clarification,
- or collaboratively updated outcomes.

Human contributors remain responsible for final interpretation and decisions.
