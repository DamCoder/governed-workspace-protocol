# Collaborative Review Standard

## Purpose of Review

Review exists to help preserve shared understanding,
alignment, and reasoning continuity across the workspace.

The review process helps identify:
- misalignment,
- reasoning gaps,
- conflicting assumptions,
- outdated context,
- and workflow drift.

The goal of review is not rigid enforcement.

The goal is helping contributors stay connected
to the shared purpose and reasoning of the workspace.

---

# Review Areas

## Purpose Alignment

Review whether work still supports
the shared purpose of the workspace.

Flag:
- disconnected work,
- unrelated expansion,
- or outputs that no longer support the intended direction.

---

## Reasoning Consistency

Review whether reasoning remains consistent across documents and stages.

Flag:
- contradictory conclusions,
- undocumented assumptions,
- or unexplained changes in direction.

---

## Trusted Source Alignment

Review whether contributors are working from
shared and trusted context.

Flag:
- unsupported claims,
- isolated assumptions,
- or conflicts with approved project documents.

---

## Workflow Continuity

Review whether work is occurring in a logical and explainable sequence.

Flag:
- skipped reasoning steps,
- premature implementation,
- or conclusions without supporting context.

---

## Context Freshness

Review whether context remains current and relevant.

Flag:
- outdated assumptions,
- abandoned work,
- obsolete guidance,
- or superseded reasoning.

---

## Explainability

Review whether contributors can reasonably understand:
- why decisions were made,
- how conclusions were reached,
- and what context influenced the outcome.

Flag:
- hidden reasoning,
- unclear transitions,
- or opaque recommendations.

---

# Review Priorities

## High Priority

Issues that could significantly impact:
- alignment,
- trust,
- understanding,
- or decision quality.

---

## Medium Priority

Issues that may create:
- confusion,
- duplicated work,
- or reduced maintainability.

---

## Low Priority

Minor opportunities to improve clarity or continuity.

---

# Human Responsibility

Review findings are guidance, not automatic decisions.

Humans remain responsible for:
- interpretation,
- discussion,
- prioritization,
- and final decisions.

---

# Review Philosophy

The purpose of collaborative review is to help maintain:
- shared understanding,
- explainable reasoning,
- contextual continuity,
- and long-term collaboration quality.
